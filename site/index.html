The idea

No daily page. No copy-forward. Just what's still open.

Most task apps make you re-create today from yesterday. Notes & Goals doesn't have a daily page at all — Today is a live query over every task you haven't finished.

Mark something done or dropped and it's gone. Leave it open and it simply keeps showing up — quietly getting older — until you deal with it. Carry-forward is automatic and invisible; there's no migration step, ever.

Some days call for more than a queue, though. Commit up to five tasks to today's slate, mixing office and personal as you like, and finish them — that's the day's only finish line. No points, no streaks, no penalties.

What's inside

Everything a day of work touches, in one quiet window.

  • The day's slate

    Commit up to five tasks to today. Finish them and the day is done — no points, no streaks, no penalties.

  • Global search — ⌘K

    Search tasks, goals, notebooks and notes — note bodies included — and jump straight to the result.

  • Notes

    A WYSIWYG editor that writes clean Markdown underneath. Headings, lists, checkboxes, quotes — nothing proprietary.

  • Goals

    Longer-term aims with progress computed live from the tasks linked to them. Nothing to update by hand.

  • Notebooks

    Context-scoped notebooks group your notes, with drag-and-drop filing. No nesting, no ceremony.

  • Activity

    A read-only look back at what you created and finished on any chosen day. No journaling required.

  • Scratchpad — ⌘J

    A floating, draggable brain-dump that lives only on your device — never in your data folder.

  • Office / Personal

    One context filter runs across every view, so work and life stay legible without ever blurring.

Your data is yours

Plain files, in a folder you choose.

No database, no server, no account. Notes & Goals writes tasks, notes, and goals as individual files — JSON for structured data, Markdown with YAML frontmatter for notes. Point the folder at Dropbox, iCloud Drive, or a git repo and it syncs itself; that's your call, not the app's.

  • No accounts
  • No cloud
  • No telemetry
data-folder/
├─ tasks/<uuid>.json      one file per task
├─ notes/<uuid>.md        YAML frontmatter + markdown body
├─ goals/<uuid>.json      one file per goal
├─ notebooks/<uuid>.json  one file per notebook
└─ .atlas/trash/          deleted files, never hard-unlinked

Install

Four steps and you're working.

  1. 1

    Download the .dmg

    Grab the latest universal build above — it runs natively on Intel and Apple Silicon.

  2. 2

    Drag to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag Notes & Goals.app into your Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Approve it once

    Double-click the app and click Done on the warning. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and confirm with your password.

  4. 4

    Choose a data folder

    Pick or create the folder where your tasks, notes, and goals will live. Change it later from Settings (⌘,).

Why the extra step? Builds aren't notarized — that needs a paid Apple Developer account this project doesn't have yet — so macOS says it can't verify the app. Open Anyway shows up in Privacy & Security for about an hour after you first try to open it, and approves the app for good. Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Notes & Goals.app" does the same. Installing a new version may ask you to approve it again; your data folder is never touched.
